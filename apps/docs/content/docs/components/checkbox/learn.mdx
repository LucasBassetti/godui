Radix renders `CheckboxPrimitive.Indicator` only while the box is checked. A
keyframe on it therefore plays at exactly the moment the check appears, with
no state to track.

The indicator reuses the shared `godui-fade-scale-in` and sets
`--godui-enter-scale: 0.5` locally, so the check grows from half size on a
snappy spring. The animation sits on the indicator `<span>`, not on the
`<svg>` inside it: Chrome won't composite the individual `scale` property on
an SVG element.

While the pointer is down, the box scales to 92% on the bouncy spring, the
same press as [Button](/docs/components/button/learn), a bit deeper because
the target is small.

shadcn's checkbox had `transition-shadow`, which animated the focus ring's
`box-shadow`. GodUI drops it: the ring and the checked fill change instantly.
Recoloring a 16px box doesn't need a tween; the pop already tells you the
state changed.

## Why GPU-only

The check animates `scale` and `opacity` on its wrapper, and the press animates
`scale`. Nothing repaints while it moves.

## Reduced motion

`--godui-motion: 0` turns the pop into a 150ms fade, and
`motion-reduce:transition-none` makes the press instant.
